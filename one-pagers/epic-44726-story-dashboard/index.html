The epic was restructured in early June: automated testing was promoted to its own feature, a Future Scope feature now holds deferred work, and a Route to Production feature gathers the deployment work — its original pipeline story (45960) is now Done under the Platform team, and it has grown into a full Azure deployment breakdown covering Key Vault and secrets, a DbUp migration runner, Azure Files image storage, App Service hardening, observability, auth, and CI/CD — those deployment stories have each been decomposed into delivery tasks, around sixty in total, from ingress and secret-delivery decisions through to slot-swap promotion. Route to Production also carries a Platform-team workstream — the API repository set-up, access and permissions, App Service Terraform, Azure File Sync, an on-prem sync agent, a .NET 9 build pipeline, the deployment pipeline, and connectivity and security. Under the field-app feature, production-parity stubs mirror the RP sign-off, mapping, offline mapping, workforce, and in-app audit work alongside the login and work-order stubs, and scope stories cover feature flags to hide non-MVP surfaces, photo limits and image size, a photo gallery, job-versus-man hours, and sign-off questions.

This is the quietest cycle the dashboard has recorded: two state changes, one assignment, and no stories added or removed. View assigned work (45888) moved from Awaiting QA into QA, joining login and permissions (45887) so the field app now has two stories actively under test. The map-tile blob storage story (49066) was picked up from Ready to Play into In Development and assigned to Keerthi Jagarlamudi, which clears the last unowned item on the epic. Nothing regressed, nothing was blocked, and no owner changed hands. The backlog holds at 95 items.
